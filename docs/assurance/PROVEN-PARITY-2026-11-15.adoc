== Hook inventory / missing adoption

`metadatastician/standards/.githooks/` has pre-commit, pre-push,
commit-msg, post-checkout, post-merge, pre-rebase, and specific K9,
SPDX, SHA-pin, actions-lock, permissions, CodeQL and bot-directives
validators. OPSM currently has `runtime/core/lefthook.ncl` but no
matching root `.githooks/` inventory on main.

This is an **integration gap**, not permission to copy scripts
unexamined. Shared validators must be pinned, tested with positive
and intentionally defective fixtures, and adapted to OPSM's Elixir,
Rust, Zig, Nickel and Idris source roots. A local hook cannot replace
PR CI: contributors can disable hooks, while CI must fail closed.

== Replacement gates — required proof before retiring any workflow

. Enumerate source files and check targets; ensure no area disappears
  from the measured denominator.
. Show the candidate gate starts and completes on a real PR, not merely
  that GitHub created a workflow run.
. Assert a passing fixture and a deliberately broken fixture for each
  substantive gate; zero jobs, missing tooling and missing reports fail
  closed or are explicitly non-enforcing.
. Pin all actions and reusable workflows to audited immutable sources,
  with valid lockfile closure; keep least-privilege token scopes.
. Record current-commit execution and reporting receipts. A property
  sample is provisional; a checked Idris2 theorem is proof only of its
  precise statement.
. Keep CI and security check contexts required by GitHub rulesets
  throughout migration. Do not merge failing changes or bypass the
  ruleset to obtain a green dashboard.
. Resolve all substantive app reviews and review threads before
  declaring a migration complete.
